What is Botmap?

Botmap is a Mac app for organizing Claude Code and Codex agents in a mind map. Each chat is a node in your project. See what your agents are doing, find the ones waiting for an answer and open the full conversation when you need the detail.

It runs your installed Claude Code and Codex command lines with the accounts you already use. Your map stays in plain files on your Mac.

How do I organize multiple AI agents?

Group chats by project and topic, then use the map to follow each agent's work. Switch between Mindmap, Swipe, Bubbles and Matrix views over the same chats.

  • Bring your existing chats. Import Claude Code and Codex conversations already on your Mac.
  • Answer what needs you. Read an agent's progress, reply to its questions and mark work done or for later.
  • Reply by voice. Dictate instructions or use GPT Live after adding your own OpenAI API key.

The Mac setup guide covers installation, importing chats and your first reply.

Before you download

What does Botmap need to run?

An Apple silicon Mac with macOS 12 or later, plus Claude Code or the Codex CLI installed and signed in. The optional terminal installer needs Node.js 18 or later. Check the setup requirements.

Is Botmap free?

Botmap is free for personal use and organizations with up to five people. Larger organizations pay USD 50 per month for the whole organization, with no per-person charge. AI provider subscriptions and API usage are separate. See the team license and billing terms.

Does Botmap replace Claude Code or Codex?

No. Botmap organizes and runs your existing agents through their own command lines. Keep your existing provider accounts and sign-ins; you do not need a separate Botmap account.

Where are my chats stored?

Botmap keeps its map and working files on your Mac. Your agents still use their AI providers, and optional dictation and live calls use OpenAI. Read the privacy policy for local storage and provider connections.

Is Botmap open source?

Botmap is source-available under the Botmap License. It is not open source. The license sets the limits on commercial use, redistribution and resale.
