Botmap

Your agents orbit.

Botmap puts every Claude Code and Codex agent on one map on your Mac, and the questions that need you in your pocket. Swipe through what is waiting, say your answer, get on with your day.

Mac

Botmap for Mac

  • Every agent on one map: Mindmap, Swipe, Bubbles and Matrix.
  • Brings in the chats already on your Mac: Claude, Codex, Hermes, OpenClaw. Nothing to sign in to.
  • Built for the keyboard, with dictation.
Download for Mac

For Macs with Apple silicon, macOS 12 or later. Needs Claude Code or Codex on the Mac; signed and notarized by Apple.

iPhone

Botmap for iPhone

  • Swipe through Needs you: left and right through a topic, up and down through your topics.
  • Answer by voice; your iPhone writes it down, on the device.
  • Talks only to your own Mac. No account, nothing collected.
