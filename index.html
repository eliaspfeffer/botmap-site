Every agent on one map.

A Mac app that puts all your Claude Code and Codex chats on one mindmap, and lets you answer them with one key or your voice.

Download for Mac

For Macs with Apple silicon, macOS 12 or later. Signed and notarized by Apple. Free.

Mac
Mindmap, Swipe, Bubbles and Matrix views. Brings in the chats already on your Mac; nothing to sign in to.
iPhone
Swipe through what needs you and answer by voice. Coming to the App Store; how pairing works.
Source
On GitHub. Free for personal use and teams of up to five, a paid licence above that.
